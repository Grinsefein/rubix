Built for trust

Speed is the easy part.

Three layers of community context keep the board useful.

01

Bring as many witnesses as you like

Every entry lists the people who saw it happen. Names are suggested from the community, but you can add anyone in plain text.

02

Anyone can raise a flag

Impossible time, invented witnesses or spam? The report button is one click away on every entry. Your IP is only stored as a one-way hash.

03

Enough reports pause the entry

After three reports from different networks an entry is hidden from the board and waits for a moderator. Dismissed reports put it right back.
